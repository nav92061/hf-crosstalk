# Analysis timeline

Every entry below is an artifact-store version timestamp, recorded automatically when the file was
written. The purpose is to let a reader check, for each pre-specification, which results already existed
when its criteria were fixed and which did not.

**This is provenance, not preregistration.** The timestamps come from the platform's own artifact store
rather than an independent third-party registry. They were not deposited with OSF, ClinicalTrials.gov or
any external timestamping service, and they cannot be retroactively made independent. A reader who
requires third-party attestation should treat these criteria as documented-in-advance rather than
preregistered. What the record does establish is ordering and content: what each criterion said, and
which results postdate it.

## Phase structure

The project ran in three phases. The first was exploratory in the ordinary sense: the screen was built
and run, and its criteria were internal to the design rather than separately registered. The second and
third phases each opened with a written pre-specification because each was testing a hypothesis
generated by an earlier result, which is exactly the situation where post-hoc criterion choice does
damage.

### An ordering point a reader should check

Four files carrying `npr1` in the name predate this pre-specification: `npr1_survival.csv`,
`npr1_proliferation.csv`, `npr1_copynumber.csv` and `fig7_npr1_exploratory.png`, written 2026-07-28 16:25 UTC to 2026-07-28 16:45 UTC.
These belong to the **separate exploratory NPR1 analysis**, which the manuscript labels exploratory and
reports under its own conjunction rule. They are not results of the PTN adversarial tests and are not
governed by this pre-specification. The distinction matters because the file-name prefix alone would
suggest otherwise.

## Pre-specification 3 — protein falsification and cross-platform replication (Sections 3.11–3.12)

| | |
|---|---|
| Written (v1) | 2026-07-29 19:27 UTC |
| Criteria final (v3) | 2026-07-29 19:36 UTC |
| Governs | three microarray cohorts, eight CPTAC proteome studies |
| First governed result | 2026-07-31 01:55 UTC (**+30.3 h** after v3) |
| Revisions | 4 versions |

This is the longest lead in the project: the criteria were final **30.3 hours** before the first result
they judge. The revision history is worth reading rather than summarising:

- **v1 → v2** (+3 min): corrected a factual error in the accompanying bias audit. I had written that all
  23 TMT plexes in the reference study contained both tumour and normal aliquots; the computed census
  showed 21 of 23, with two plexes holding 9 tumours and no normals. The pre-specification gained a
  binding clause forbidding single-arm plexes from entering any unpaired analysis without plex as a
  blocking factor.
- **v2 → v3** (+6 min): added Section 2.3b, distinguishing which tumour types permit a direct
  transcript-versus-protein contrast from those where the protein result stands alone.
- **v3 → v4** (+1.2 h, i.e. *after* results): corrected the justification cited for requiring an
  abundance-matched null. The text had cited the expression-floor contrast (ρ 0.363 vs 0.107) when the
  relevant comparison is the matching contrast (ρ 0.323 unmatched vs 0.363 matched). **This changed a
  citation, not a criterion.** No threshold, no test and no verdict rule was altered, and the verdicts
  had already been computed under the v3 criteria. A reader checking whether criteria moved after results
  should read v3 against v4 directly.

## What had been seen before criteria were fixed

The pre-specification for phase 3 declares this itself, and it is the honest weak point. Verifying the
proteomics controls required computing on real data, so an effect direction in one tumour type — clear
cell renal carcinoma — had been observed before the criteria were written. That type is designated
non-blind throughout, its result is reported separately from the blind types, and the kill criterion was
stated in terms of the seven types that had not been examined. Two of those seven proved testable against
a required minimum of four, and the track was abandoned as underpowered.
